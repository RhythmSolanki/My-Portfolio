FAQ

How long does it take to build a website?

Timeline depends on scope. Landing pages typically ship in about 7 days, business sites in around 10 days, and full builds in up to 14 days. Many projects finish within a 3–7 day average when requirements are clear from the start.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops for a consistent, responsive experience.

Can I request changes during the project?

Absolutely. Each package includes revision rounds (2 for Landing Page, 3 for Business Website and Full Build). I keep the process transparent so feedback is easy to incorporate.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Website maintenance covers updates, performance monitoring, security patches, and content changes. Ongoing support is available so your site stays fast and secure after launch.

What do you need to get started?

A short call or email about your goals, any brand assets or copy you have, and examples you like. I handle the technical setup and guide you through anything that's missing.

Do the prices include domain and hosting?

No. Domain and hosting costs are separate from the project prices listed above. I can recommend reliable options and help you connect everything at launch.
